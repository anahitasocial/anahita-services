# Making it your own

[Documentation](README.md) › Making it your own

The pages for changing how a site looks, what it says and what it allows,
without changing the code.

1. **Its name and look.** The name and description:
   [Site identity](configuration.md#site-identity) here, and in the web app,
   [Name and description](https://github.com/anahitasocial/anahita-web/blob/main/docs/customising.md#name-and-description).
   Logo, colours, fonts and the home page visitors see:
   [Themes](https://github.com/anahitasocial/anahita-web/blob/main/docs/customising.md#themes).
2. **Its languages.**
   [Languages](https://github.com/anahitasocial/anahita-web/blob/main/docs/customising.md#languages)
   in the web app. What a post's language is used for:
   [The language of a post](post-language.md).
3. **Its legal texts.** Your Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, and asking
   everybody to accept a new version:
   [Terms of Service and Privacy Policy](https://github.com/anahitasocial/anahita-web/blob/main/docs/customising.md#terms-of-service-and-privacy-policy)
   in the web app, and [the server's side](registration.md#terms-of-service-and-privacy-policy).
4. **Who may do what, by default.** Who may post, reply, like and add
   followers on each kind of profile: [The defaults](permissions.md#the-defaults),
   and [what profile owners can change](permissions.md#what-profile-owners-can-change).
5. **Who may join, and what visitors see.**
   [Registration modes](registration.md#registration-modes), and the
   [privacy settings](privacy.md#what-the-installation-allows) of the whole
   installation.
6. **How search ranks.** The weights, and how fast the past fades:
   [Tuning it](search.md#tuning-it).
7. **The reasons for a report.** Your own list in place of the built-in one:
   [Using your own reasons](abuse-reports.md#using-your-own-reasons).
8. **How long things are kept.** Unused accounts, deleted ones, and the
   archives people download:
   [Account cleanup](configuration.md#account-cleanup),
   [Account lifecycle](account-lifecycle.md),
   [Download your data](data-export.md#keeping-and-deleting).

Not written yet: starting configurations for kinds of site (a school, a
newsroom, a library), planned in [recipes.md](plans/recipes.md).
