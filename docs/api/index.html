Abuse reports abuse-report-service

Reporting a post, a reply or a profile, and the administrators' queue of what was reported. Optional: an installation may not run it (abuse-reports.md).

POST /abuse-reports Report something

Nobody is told: the person reported never learns of the report or who made it, and administrators find it in their queue. A thing the viewer cannot see answers exactly as one that does not exist, so that reporting is no way to find out what is hidden. Twenty reports an hour at most.

Who: somebody signed in, with account:write

Takes, as application/json

FieldTypeWhat it is
detailsstringWhat the reporter adds in their own words. Needed where the reason says so. At most 1000 characters.
reason_key alwaysstringWhy, by the reason's key (GET /abuse-reports/reasons). At most 64 characters.
target_id alwaysintegerThe id of what is reported: a post, a reply, a profile. At least 1.

Answers

200the viewer had reported it already; their report was updated
201reported
400own_content, it is the viewer's own; reason_not_applicable, the reason is not for this kind of thing; details_required, the reason needs the reporter's own words; or not well formed, without a body
Refusal application/json
401no token, or one that has expired
403the viewer may not do this
404there is no such thing that the viewer can see
429too many reports in the last hour

Answered by abusereport/cmd/api/handlers.(*reportsHandler).Add
